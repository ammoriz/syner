SYNER is attendance for leaders who know their team by name. Shifts, leave and overtime for any way of working, kept privately on your iPhone.

Get helpHow your data is kept

From synergy: more together than alone.

SYNER keeps track of who is in, who is resting and who gave extra hours, so you can look up from the list and see the people.

One touch, twice a day

Mark each person present at the start of the shift and again at the end. Times are the full shift unless you change them.

Every way of working

Five and six day weeks, short and long rotations, any number of groups, day and night shifts, and a different overtime for each day.

The full picture of each day

On duty, Leave, Sick, Excused, Course or Holiday. Leave is set once for the whole period.

Hours you can trust

Regular hours and overtime are counted separately, day by day. Add or take away overtime for any single day.

Reports ready to share

A clear monthly sheet for the whole team or for one person, as PDF or CSV.

Hand over safely

Pass your data, or one employee, to another leader in an encrypted file that opens only with a code.

A month at a glance.

Every person has a calendar of the month and the totals that matter: days worked, regular hours and overtime.

  • Permanent and assignmentAn assignment has a start and an end, and leaves the list by itself when it is over.
  • Changes from a dateWhen a pattern or a group changes, earlier days stay exactly as they were.
  • Nothing counted unfairlyA rest day is never an absence. A day that was not recorded is never an absence.

One sheet for the whole month.

Every day, every status and every hour of overtime on a single page, ready to print or send. On iPhone Duo it fills the open screen.

Private by design.

What you know about your people stays with you.

On your iPhone only

Everything is stored on the device. There is no server and no cloud copy.

No account

No sign-up, no email, no password. Open the app and start.

No ads, no tracking

SYNER collects nothing and contains no analytics or advertising.
