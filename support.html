Support

Answers to the questions that come up most. New to SYNER? Start with the step-by-step guide.

Getting started

How do I set up my team?

Open Settings, then Work Patterns, and add the way your team works: a five or six day week, or a rotation. Then open Employees and add each person, choosing their pattern and group.

How do I record a day?

On Today, touch the first circle beside a person when they arrive and the second when the shift ends. All Start and All End mark everyone at once. Touch a name to set a status, the actual times or a note.

Can I record a day I missed?

Yes. Use the arrows beside the date on Today, or touch the date to pick any earlier day. Days in the future cannot be recorded.

Work patterns

My team works in rotating groups. How do I set that up?

Add a pattern, set the length of the cycle, mark the work days and add a group for each crew. Each group has a start date that says when it is on the first day of the cycle.

What happens when I change a pattern or move someone to another group?

The app asks from which date the change applies. Earlier days stay as they were, and only days from that date follow the new arrangement.

Hours and overtime

How are regular hours and overtime worked out?

Each shift has its regular hours, eight by default. Hours beyond them are overtime. You can give each day of the cycle its own overtime in the pattern. A day that was not worked earns no overtime.

Can I change the overtime for one day?

Yes. Open the day for that person and use the plus and minus under Overtime. This is available on On duty days. Reset goes back to what the day's times give.

Leave and status

How do I record leave?

Open the day, choose Leave and pick the last day. Every scheduled day in that period is shown as Leave. Choosing On duty on a day inside the period ends the leave there.

Reports

How do I share a report?

Open Reports, choose the month, and open the group report or one person's report. The share button sends it as a PDF. The monthly totals can also be exported as a CSV file.

Your data

Where is my data kept?

Only on your iPhone. SYNER has no account and no server. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings, then Backup, and export a backup file. Keep it somewhere safe: a backup file is not encrypted. To bring it back, choose Restore on the same screen.

How do I hand my team over to someone else?

Open Settings, then Transfer. Choose Hand over everything if someone is taking over your position for good, or Transfer an employee for one person. Send the encrypted file, and give the other person the code separately. They open the file in SYNER, or under Transfer, Receive. A full handover also carries the teams you follow and your link with a supervisor.

I lost the transfer code.

The code cannot be recovered, and the file cannot be opened without it. Make a new transfer from the iPhone that still has the data.

I got a new iPhone. How do I move my data?

Export a backup on the old iPhone, send the file to the new one, and restore it there from Settings, Backup.

Working with a supervisor

How do I share my team with my supervisor?

Open Settings, then Team Sharing. Name your team, create the link, send the link file and give your supervisor the code separately. That is done once. After that, touch the send button beside search on Today whenever you want to send an update; there is no code. The guide shows each screen.

What can my supervisor see and do?

Everything you hold about your team: attendance, hours, leave, notes, and each person's details. They can look at all of it and change none of it. Only you record your team.

I am a supervisor. How do I open a file a leader sent me?

Open the file and choose SYNER. From WhatsApp, open the file, touch the share button and pick SYNER. You can also open Settings, Team Sharing, Open a team file. The first file from a leader asks for their code; later files do not.

The app says I already have a newer update.

You opened a file older than the one you already hold, so nothing changed. If a leader moved to a new iPhone or restored a backup and their files keep being refused, remove the team in Settings, Team Sharing, and ask the leader for a new link file.

Do the leaders' teams change my own numbers?

No. The counters on Today, All Start and All End, the Employees tab and your own reports are for your own team only. The teams you follow sit under the Teams heading on Today, and the All teams report in Reports puts everyone on one sheet.

How do I stop sharing, or stop following a team?

A leader opens Settings, Team Sharing, Stop sharing. The supervisor keeps what they already have; it stops updating. A supervisor removes a team from the same screen, or from the menu at the top of the team.

Covering for a colleague

I am going away. How does a colleague record my team?

Open Settings, Transfer, Hand over for cover. Name your team, create the file, send it and give the code separately. Creating the file locks your team on your iPhone, so only your colleague records it while you are away. See the guide.

Why is my team locked?

Because a colleague is covering it. With one phone recording at a time, nothing is lost when the team comes back. You can still read everything. It unlocks when you open the return file your colleague sends.

How do I get my team back?

Ask your colleague to open the covered team, then Settings, Transfer, Return this team, and to send you the file and its code. Open the file in SYNER, enter the code and touch Take my team back.

The cover file never reached my colleague.

Open Settings, Transfer and choose Cancel the cover to unlock your team, then start again. Cancel only if your colleague has not started covering: a return sent after a cancel is not accepted.

I am covering a team. Where is it?

At the top of Today there is a switch between My team and the team you are covering. While a covered team is selected, every tab works on that team. Your own team is not touched.

Why can't I erase my iPhone or hand everything over?

You are still covering a colleague's team. Return it first, then remove it from your iPhone.
