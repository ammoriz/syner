Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

The short version: SYNER collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud Drive if you switch on iCloud backup. We never see it.

What SYNER stores

SYNER is an attendance app. To do its job it stores what you type into it: the names and details of your employees, their work patterns, their daily attendance, leave, notes and hours. All of it is saved on your iPhone, inside the app. If you switch on iCloud backup, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud Drive, as described below.

What we collect

Nothing. SYNER has no account, no sign-up and no server. It does not send your data to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking tools.

When data leaves your iPhone

Data leaves the app only when you choose to send it yourself, or when you have switched on iCloud backup:

  • Reports. You can export a report as a PDF or CSV file and share it with any app you choose.
  • Backups. You can export a backup file and keep it wherever you like. A backup file is not encrypted, so store it somewhere safe.
  • iCloud backup. This is off unless you switch it on in Settings, Backup. While it is on, the app saves a backup file into the SYNER folder of your own iCloud Drive, at most once a day after something has changed, and keeps the last 7 days. The files hold the same as an exported backup and are not encrypted by SYNER. They are stored by Apple in your iCloud account, under Apple's privacy policy and your own settings; they never pass through us, and we cannot read them. Switch it off at any time; backups already in iCloud Drive stay until you delete them in the Files app.
  • Transfers. You can hand your data, or one employee, to another person in a transfer file, for good or for a period of cover. Transfer files are encrypted and open only with a code that the app shows you. Share the code separately from the file.
  • Team updates. A leader can share their team's record with a supervisor. The first file is encrypted and opens only with a code; later files are encrypted with a key that only the two iPhones hold. A team update carries everything the leader keeps about the team, including each person's details and notes, and the supervisor can read all of it.

Files are sent with the apps you choose, such as WhatsApp, Mail or AirDrop. SYNER has no server of its own and none of these files pass through us. Once a file has left the app, whoever receives it is responsible for it.

Device backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, the app's data may be part of that backup. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and your own settings.

The people in your list

The information you keep about your employees is yours to look after. Keep only what you need, and follow the rules of your workplace and your country about employee records. This applies equally to a team you follow as a supervisor and to a team you cover for a colleague: their records are on your iPhone while you do.

Deleting your data

You can delete any employee from inside the app, which removes their records as well. A team you follow can be removed in Settings, Team Sharing. A team you cover is removed when you return it. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes everything it stored on the iPhone. Backups you exported, and backups kept in your iCloud Drive, are files of yours: delete them where you keep them, for example in the Files app.

Children

SYNER is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
